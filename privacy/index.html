Contact information

If you call, email or message Ross, the details you provide are used to respond to your enquiry, discuss work and manage any agreed project. Do not send unnecessary sensitive information.

Website and third-party services

This website uses no active analytics or marketing cookies in its default configuration. The site loads a map from Google Maps and a web font from Google Fonts; these providers may receive connection information such as your IP address. WhatsApp, email and external map links open separate services with their own privacy policies. The hosting provider may process technical logs for security and service operation.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal obligations. You can contact Ross at rossdoherty2003@gmail.com. You may also contact Ireland’s Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.

Retention and security

Enquiry and project information should be retained only as needed for the enquiry, agreed work and applicable legal obligations. Contact Ross for information about a specific record.
